Meet the Animal Groups

Explore different types of animals and learn something new.

🦁

Mammals

Lions, elephants, dogs and many other animals are mammals.

🦅

Birds

Eagles, parrots, penguins and owls are examples of birds.

🐠

Fish

Fish live in water and use gills to breathe.

🐍

Reptiles

Snakes, turtles, lizards and crocodiles are reptiles.

🐸

Amphibians

Frogs and salamanders are amphibians that often live near water.

🦋

Insects

Butterflies, bees and ants are insects with six legs.
